The Theory of Constraints

Matchbook definition

A system can only go as fast as its slowest step. Find that step, improve it first, and the whole system speeds up.

Presented bySerena YangFall 2026
Live line · 4 work stations System output = 16 units / hr = the slowest step
01Where it comes fromReq. 2 · TheoristReq. 3 · Primary journal reference

A physicist wrote a novel about a factory

Every organization has at least one constraint that limits its performance. TOC says managers should aim their improvement effort at that constraint, instead of improving every department on its own.

Original theorist

Eliyahu M. Goldratt

1947 – 2011 · Israeli physicist and management thinker

Goldratt brought TOC to a broad business audience through The Goal (1984), a business novel co-written with Jeff Cox. A plant manager has three months to save his failing factory and learns that the plant's output is set by its bottlenecks.

Key idea from The Goal

A bottleneck is a resource whose capacity can't meet the demand placed on it. An hour lost at the bottleneck is an hour lost for the whole system.

  1. 1984

    The Goal

    Goldratt & Cox introduce the bottleneck idea to managers through a story.

  2. 1987

    The Theory of Constraints Journal

    Goldratt publishes the theory's ideas in his own journal, starting with hierarchical management and its inherent conflict.

  3. 2007

    Academic review

    Watson et al. trace how TOC grew from a scheduling method into a full management philosophy.

Primary journal reference

Goldratt, E. M. (1987). Chapter 1: Hierarchical management—The inherent conflict. The Theory of Constraints Journal, 1(1), 1–17.

05How the theory is testedReq. 7 · Survey / observation details

Measure it, then measure again

Researchers use numbers and observation. Surveys check whether firms that use TOC practices perform better. Before-and-after studies measure what changes once the five steps are applied.

  1. 01 Sample61 plants

    European manufacturing firms that had implemented TOC.

  2. 02 Respondents7 per plant

    Supervisors plus production, inventory and supply-chain managers.

  3. 03 Instrument7-point Likert

    Questionnaire rating how fully each TOC practice is in place.

  4. 04 AnalysisANOVA + regression

    Tests the link between TOC practices and manufacturing performance.

  5. 05 FindingSchedule the constraint

    A constraint-based master schedule and spare capacity at non-constraints mattered most.
